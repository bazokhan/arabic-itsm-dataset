# Expert-audit release materials

This directory contains privacy-safe materials for reproducing and inspecting the expert quality audit:

- `annotation_template.csv`: blank rating-sheet schema;
- `iaa_results.json`: aggregate inter-rater agreement results;
- `../scripts/sample_for_annotation.py`: deterministic proportional-L1 sampling and the complete rating rubric;
- `../scripts/compute_iaa.py`: aggregate agreement computation.

Completed individual rating sheets are intentionally withheld. The participant consent terms permit aggregate reporting and do not authorize publication of identifiable or row-level individual responses. Signed consent records, if retained by the investigators, must remain private and must not be committed to this repository.
